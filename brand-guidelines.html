Aluvity brand guidelines

Everything needed to place the Aluvity logo, colours and type correctly. Download the logo kit for the files shown here.

The symbol

Two soft, curved ribbons rise toward each other to form an abstract A, with a teal circle at the centre. Two paths come together to create access to care, with the person at the middle of the journey. The curves are never straight diagonals, and the symbol is never redrawn as a conventional letter A. The teal curve overlaps the indigo curve at the crown and closes the arch beneath it, so the underside of the A reads as one continuous curve.

Left curve and circle

Aluvity Teal. Care, access, connection.

Right curve

Aluvity Indigo on light backgrounds, white on dark. Trust and stability.

Logo suite

Four lockups, all built from the same curved A-symbol. Pick by available space, not by taste.

Primary (horizontal)

Symbol and name side by side. The default logo: website header, app, email, documents, social.

Stacked

Symbol above the name. Presentations, posters, square and narrow spaces.

Symbol

The mark alone. App and social icons, favicons, spaces where the name is already shown.

Horizontal with tagline

For hero sections, print, decks and signage when there is room for the tagline.

Stacked with tagline

Centred version with tagline. Covers, posters, pitch decks.

Using the tagline

The core logo is the default. Add the tagline only when there is room and it adds something.

  • Left-aligned under the name in the horizontal lockup, centred in the stacked lockup. Never stretched to the width of the name.
  • Indigo on light backgrounds. Teal only on indigo or other dark backgrounds, where it has enough contrast.
  • Plus Jakarta Sans Medium with slightly open spacing. Do not change the font, colour or position, and do not build it into the wordmark.
  • Use the core logo whenever the tagline would fall below the minimum sizes below. The tagline can also be set separately as campaign copy.

App and favicon icons

The symbol sits on indigo, teal or a light tile. Use the full-bleed files for app stores and let the platform round the corners.

Visual language

The rest of the identity borrows the logo's curves without turning every element into one.

  • Mostly white and light neutral backgrounds. Indigo for major brand elements, teal for meaningful accents and interactions, near black for body text.
  • Rounded cards, gentle arcs, soft connected shapes and generous whitespace.
  • Indigo backgrounds for selected high-impact sections only.
  • Calm and clean: no heavy gradients, glows or glassmorphism.

Please don't

  • Recolour the symbol or swap the indigo and teal.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows and effects.
  • Set the name in any font other than the supplied artwork.
  • Place the colour logo on busy images or low-contrast backgrounds. Switch to reversed or one-colour.
  • Add medical crosses, hearts, stethoscopes, pills or other literal healthcare imagery to the logo.
  • Replace the curved ribbons with straight diagonal lines.
  • Redraw, trace or re-cut the symbol. Where the teal and indigo curves meet is part of the mark, so always use the supplied artwork.
  • Use teal text for small copy on white. It does not meet contrast requirements.
